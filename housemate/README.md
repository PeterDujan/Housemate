# Housemate

Home Assistant app: a voice-first assistant that lives in your house. Its brain, its ears and its
voice in one app, each house with its own people, personality and settings.
Runs on 64-bit Intel or AMD boxes only: not on a Raspberry Pi or other ARM boards yet.
What it does: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md). User docs: [DOCS.md](DOCS.md).
